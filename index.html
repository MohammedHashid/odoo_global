About Us

Industry-Focused Odoo ERP Solutions That Drive Growth

At Prajai Technologies, we specialize in delivering tailored Odoo ERP solutions designed to match the unique workflows of different industries. Our goal is to help businesses streamline operations, gain real-time visibility, and scale with confidence using a unified ERP platform.
Manufacturing
We implement end-to-end Odoo ERP solutions for manufacturing businesses—covering production planning, Bills of Materials (BoM), work orders, quality control, and maintenance—enabling complete visibility across the shop floor.
Retail & Supermarkets
Our Odoo retail solutions integrate POS, inventory, pricing, promotions, and loyalty programs—allowing seamless management of single or multi-store retail operations from one system.
